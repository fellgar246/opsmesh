## budget-limit

Extended cost must be less than or equal to the available purchasing budget. If the budget cannot cover the minimum order quantity, the proposal is infeasible.

## approval-thresholds

A purchase total greater than 5000 currency units requires manager approval.
A purchase total greater than 25000 currency units also requires finance approval.
